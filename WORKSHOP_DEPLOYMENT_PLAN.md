# Workshop Deployment Plan — 40 Hours

This plan is participant-facing and can be delivered as five eight-hour days or ten four-hour studios.

## Phase 1 — Qualify and baseline | Hours 1–5

- Run package preflight and brownfield diagnostics.
- Read the case and map stakeholders, decision rights and prohibited actions.
- Establish current-state KPIs, value leakage and no-AI alternatives.
- Create the assumptions, evidence-authority and issue registers.

**Exit evidence:** baseline, problem framing, source-authority hypothesis and initial risk register.

## Phase 2 — Discover the domain | Hours 6–11

- Build the DDD context map and ubiquitous language.
- Reconcile identifiers, temporal rules, jurisdictions and data lineage.
- Decide whether a knowledge graph is justified for candidate multi-hop questions.
- Define ontology and semantic-layer ownership.

**Exit evidence:** bounded contexts, data contracts, ontology, semantic definitions and graph decision.

## Phase 3 — Specify the product | Hours 12–16

- Define intended purpose, users, affected persons and human-review model.
- Write functional, non-functional, security, privacy, fairness and evaluation requirements.
- Trace all 96 injects to requirements and tests.
- Define three workflow input/output contracts and abstention behaviour.

**Exit evidence:** product blueprint, requirements traceability and acceptance gates.

## Phase 4 — Architect and build | Hours 17–27

- Produce C4 views and at least 12 ADRs.
- Isolate or replace the defective brownfield starter.
- Implement the three workflows under `submission/`.
- Add authorization, tenant, purpose, provenance, idempotency and human-approval controls.
- Implement one-command setup, run, test and reset paths.

**Exit evidence:** runnable POC, contracts, architecture and engineering evidence.

## Phase 5 — Break, evaluate and recover | Hours 28–35

- Execute all 18 public fixtures.
- Add golden, edge, adversarial, fairness, multilingual, performance and cost tests.
- Demonstrate prompt injection and tool-manifest poisoning resistance.
- Exercise regional outage, replay, fallback regression and AI-disabled continuity.

**Exit evidence:** evaluation report, release gates, incident evidence and recovery runbook.

## Phase 6 — Govern, hand over and defend | Hours 36–40

- Complete regulatory, ISO/IEC 42001-aligned, vendor and operating-model artefacts.
- Quantify unit economics, residual risk and benefits controls.
- Run strict final-submission validation.
- Rehearse the 25-minute evidence-led defence and participant-authored elevator pitch.

**Exit evidence:** complete submission, 90-day roadmap, handover acceptance and go/pivot/pause/stop recommendation.
