# Offline Regulatory and Standards Guide

**Verification baseline:** 1 August 2026. This is a research aid, not legal advice. Applicability must be determined by legal entity, jurisdiction, product, workflow, intended purpose and role in the AI value chain.

## Insurance-sector AI supervision

### IAIS Application Paper on supervision of artificial intelligence — July 2025

Use as a supervisory principles anchor for governance, risk management, conduct, consumer outcomes and prudential soundness. It applies existing Insurance Core Principles to AI rather than replacing jurisdiction-specific law.

### EIOPA Opinion on AI governance and risk management — August 2025

Use for EU insurance-sector expectations on proportionate AI governance, fairness, data governance, documentation, record keeping, human oversight, accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity. Participants must separately determine EU AI Act classification and insurance-sector obligations.

### NAIC Model Bulletin on the Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems by Insurers

Use as a US state-regulatory governance anchor. Adoption and implementation vary by state; participants must not treat the model bulletin as uniformly binding across all US jurisdictions.

### IRDAI Information and Cyber Security Guidelines, 2023

Use as an India cyber-governance anchor for insurers. Participants must verify current circulars, amendments, entity scope and policyholder-protection obligations before asserting compliance.

## Cross-cutting anchors

### EU AI Act

Classify each workflow by intended purpose, role and affected context. Document prohibited-practice checks, transparency, literacy, deployer/provider responsibilities and any high-risk analysis. Do not assume every insurance AI use is high-risk or low-risk without analysis.

### DORA

For in-scope EU financial entities, assess ICT risk management, incident handling, resilience testing, third-party dependencies, contractual controls and exit. Avoid claiming DORA applies identically to every group entity.

### Solvency II and actuarial governance

Treat reserve, capital and model-use outputs as controlled actuarial processes. The POC may prepare evidence or scenarios but must not issue actuarial opinions, alter reserves or make capital decisions.

### FCA general-insurance pricing and product governance

For relevant UK general-insurance products, consider pricing practices, product governance, fair value and customer outcomes. Applicability differs by product and activity.

### ISO/IEC 42001 alignment

Use the management-system lens for policy, roles, objectives, risk assessment, impact assessment, lifecycle controls, competence, communication, documented information, monitoring, audit and continual improvement. “Aligned” does not mean certified.

## Required applicability record

For every cited source record:

- legal entity and jurisdiction;
- product and workflow;
- intended purpose and AI role;
- binding requirement, supervisory expectation, internal policy or design choice;
- accountable interpretation owner;
- evidence required;
- uncertainty and review date.

The official research anchors remain in `sources/01_*.md` through `sources/09_*.md`. `sources/SOURCE_VERIFICATION.csv` records the official URLs checked for this release; participants must re-verify them when used after the package baseline date.
