# Participant Artefact Expectations

The submission must contain completed versions of all 32 templates and a reproducible implementation of the three mandatory workflows.

## Non-negotiable evidence

- Quantified baseline, no-AI comparison and benefits-realisation design.
- Explicit source-authority, temporal-applicability and jurisdiction model.
- DDD, ontology and semantic layer aligned to policy, party, claim, coverage, peril, event and treaty concepts.
- Knowledge-graph decision based on query need rather than fashion.
- C4 architecture and at least 12 meaningful ADRs.
- Working coverage/claim reconciliation, underwriting/pricing support and catastrophe/reinsurance planning workflows.
- Structured outputs that separate fact, inference, conflict, missing evidence, recommendation and prohibited action.
- Current authorization, tenant isolation, purpose limitation and human approval.
- Security evidence covering prompt injection, tool poisoning, cross-tenant leakage, replay and denial-of-wallet.
- Fairness, explainability, accessibility, multilingual and contestability evidence.
- Evaluation and TEVV across accuracy, coverage fidelity, policy citation, fairness, security, reliability, latency and cost.
- Token efficiency, unit economics and AI FinOps controls.
- Manual fallback, model substitution, vendor exit, retirement and evidence preservation.
- Operational handover and successful final defence.

## Implementation constraint

The supplied repository is offline and dependency-light. Participants may replace or extend the starter code, but the final package must include one-command setup, run, test, evaluation and reset paths. No live customer, policy, claim or medical data may be introduced.
