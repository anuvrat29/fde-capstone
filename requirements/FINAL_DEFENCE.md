# Final Defence Requirements

The defence is a 25-minute evidence-led session:

1. **Executive case — 3 minutes:** problem, no-AI alternative, value, risk and recommendation.
2. **Domain and product — 3 minutes:** bounded contexts, users, decision rights and prohibited actions.
3. **Architecture — 4 minutes:** data authority, ontology, semantic layer, graph decision, C4 and ADRs.
4. **Three workflow demonstration — 7 minutes:** happy path, conflict, abstention and human approval.
5. **Attack and recovery — 3 minutes:** prompt/tool attack, containment, degraded mode and replay protection.
6. **Evaluation and economics — 3 minutes:** release gates, subgroup results, latency, token and unit cost.
7. **Handover and pitch — 2 minutes:** operating ownership, 90-day roadmap, 60-second pitch and stop conditions.

The panel may select any disclosed inject, public scenario, policy version, user role or model endpoint. The team must trace the answer to evidence and state what the system is not authorized to decide.
