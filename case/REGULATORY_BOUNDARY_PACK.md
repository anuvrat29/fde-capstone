# Regulatory Boundary and Research Pack

This pack supplies research anchors, not legal conclusions. Participants must determine applicability by entity, jurisdiction, product, workflow and intended purpose.

## Insurance and AI supervision

- IAIS Application Paper on supervision of artificial intelligence, July 2025.
- EIOPA Opinion on AI governance and risk management, August 2025.
- NAIC Model Bulletin on the Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems by Insurers and state adoption analysis.
- IRDAI information and cyber-security expectations and policyholder-protection requirements.
- UK FCA insurance pricing, product governance, fair value and consumer-outcome expectations.

## Cross-cutting boundaries

- EU AI Act applicability and high-risk analysis.
- GDPR and relevant local privacy law.
- DORA where applicable to EU financial entities and ICT dependencies.
- Solvency II, IFRS 17, local reserving and capital governance.
- Sanctions, anti-fraud, complaint, ombudsman and records-retention duties.
- State- or jurisdiction-specific automated-decision, adverse-action and unfair-discrimination obligations.

## Required output

Participants must produce an applicability matrix that distinguishes legal requirement, supervisory expectation, internal policy and design choice. They must document uncertainty and identify qualified owners for final interpretation.
