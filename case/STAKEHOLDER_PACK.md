# Stakeholder Evidence Pack

| Stakeholder | Objective | Tension | Decision authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group CEO | Restore service and protect trust | Wants visible speed during catastrophe | Enterprise funding only |
| Group Chief Claims Officer | Faster evidence reconciliation | Rejects autonomous coverage or payment | Claims operating standards |
| Chief Actuary | Stable reserves and model governance | Cat-model and inflation assumptions conflict | Actuarial opinion and reserving governance |
| India Claims Head | Hardship support and local compliance | Core systems are degraded | Local claim authority matrix |
| EU Data Protection Officer | Purpose and minimisation | Group model training expands data use | Privacy escalation and advice |
| US Chief Underwriter | Growth and explainable underwriting | External data and state variation | Underwriting authority by state/product |
| Reinsurance Director | Protect recoveries and liquidity | Event and treaty wording are disputed | Notice and recovery governance |
| Head of Special Investigations | Disrupt organised fraud | Model labels and household links are unreliable | Investigation referral, not claim outcome |
| Customer Advocate | Fair, accessible and contestable outcomes | Automation omits favourable wording | Customer-outcome escalation |
| CISO | Contain ransomware and tool compromise | Business wants rapid restoration | Security containment and access control |
| AI Product Owner | Deliver three workflows | Pressure to hide limitations | Product scope only |
| Third-party Adjuster Lead | Restore field access | Revoked users remain cached | No internal payment authority |

## Interview extracts

- Claims: “Do not collapse a coverage conflict into a confidence score.”
- Actuarial: “A fast wrong event aggregation can distort both reserves and reinsurance.”
- Customer Advocate: “The customer must be able to challenge the evidence and the rule, not merely the prose.”
- CISO: “A read-only tool that can mutate a payee is not read-only.”
- Reinsurance: “The ceded answer depends on signed wording, peril, hours and currency date—not the claim system label.”
- Product: “The POC must prove abstention and safe degradation, not just a successful happy path.”
