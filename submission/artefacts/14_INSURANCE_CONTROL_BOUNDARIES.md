# 14 Insurance Control Boundaries

## Purpose

Binding, pricing, claim, reserve, payment and treaty authorities and non-bypassable controls for the three workflows, consolidating the prohibited-action lists already established in 03/04/10/11 into one per-action authority matrix, and grounding the payment and reserve boundaries in confirmed defects (INJ-027 reserve leakage, INJ-036 emergency payment pressure, INJ-048 insider claim manipulation).

## Acceptance criteria

- The artefact is evidence-led and references supplied paths, record locators and inject IDs.
- Facts, assumptions, inference, conflict, decision and residual risk are distinguishable.
- Temporal, jurisdictional, authorization and authority boundaries are explicit where relevant.
- Owners, approvals, review triggers and measurable acceptance gates are named.
- The artefact contains no unsupported coverage, pricing, reserve, payment or treaty conclusion.

## Artefact-specific acceptance criteria

- Every regulated action (bind, decline, price, reserve, settle, pay, cancel, repudiate, place treaty, submit recovery) has exactly one row naming the AI-permitted preparatory step, the explicit prohibition, the human authority, the evidence required, and the audit-event reference (CTRL-ICB-01).
- No row states an AI-prepared output as sufficient authority on its own; every row requires a named human role's action to convert preparation into an operational step (CTRL-ICB-02).
- The payment-authority row explicitly reflects knowledge/PAYMENT_CONTROL_POLICY.md's four-eyes and idempotency requirement (CTRL-ICB-03).
- The reserve-authority row explicitly reflects the confirmed INJ-027 defect (batch job overwrote reserves with model suggestions) as a violation this boundary must prevent from recurring (CTRL-ICB-04).

## Alternatives and trade-offs

| Option | Benefits | Risks | Cost/complexity | Reversibility | Decision and rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reuse the existing board-level prohibited-action list (INJ-005) as-is without adding action-specific evidence/audit-event detail | Lower documentation effort | Would not satisfy DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md's requirement for controls with named owners, approvals and measurable acceptance gates; would leave INJ-027/INJ-036/INJ-048's specific failure patterns unaddressed at the control-design level | Lower cost now, higher risk of an ineffective control at implementation | Reversible but wasteful | Rejected. The board list states what is prohibited; this artefact must additionally state what evidence and audit trail make the boundary enforceable and auditable, per CTRL-ICB-01. |
| Design a single generic "human-approval-required" flag for all ten regulated actions, without action-specific evidence or audit-event requirements | Simpler schema | Cannot express the different evidence needed per action (e.g., sanctions-screening for payments vs. delegated-authority confirmation for binding); would not have caught the specific INJ-027/INJ-036/INJ-048 patterns, which each require a distinct control shape | Lower initial cost, high residual risk of the exact failure patterns already disclosed in the case evidence | Costly to reverse once a generic gate is embedded across all ten actions | Rejected. A generic flag cannot satisfy A-02/A-03/A-04's requirement for pattern-specific triggers (reserve pre/post-value logging; payee-change-timing trigger; incomplete-check override justification). |
| Ten action-specific rows, each naming the AI-permitted preparation, explicit prohibition, human authority, evidence required and audit event, with three rows (reserve, payment, hardship-advance) carrying an additional pattern-specific control closing a confirmed or disclosed defect | Directly closes INJ-027, INJ-036 and INJ-048's specific failure patterns; reuses the already-established board prohibited-action list rather than redefining it; gives every regulated action a concrete, testable audit-event requirement | Ten rows to maintain instead of one; requires coordination with 03/04's existing CTRL-SDR-01/CTRL-PSB-01/02/03 to avoid drift | Medium — ten rows, each independently testable | Reversible; any row can be revised without affecting the others | Selected. Satisfies CTRL-ICB-01 through CTRL-ICB-04 and directly addresses the three confirmed/disclosed defect patterns with pattern-specific controls, not generic ones. |
