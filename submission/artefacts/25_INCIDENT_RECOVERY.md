# 25 Incident Recovery

## Purpose

AI/security/claims incident response, evidence preservation, correction and recovery for the three workflows, grounded in the confirmed ransomware containment event (DOWN-01/network_zones.csv) and the concrete continuity requirements already supplied in data/continuity_requirements.csv, closing the manual-mode-procedure gap deferred from 13_MODEL_PROMPT_LIFECYCLE.md and 24_RELIABILITY_OBSERVABILITY.md.

## Acceptance criteria

- The artefact is evidence-led and references supplied paths, record locators and inject IDs.
- Facts, assumptions, inference, conflict, decision and residual risk are distinguishable.
- Temporal, jurisdictional, authorization and authority boundaries are explicit where relevant.
- Owners, approvals, review triggers and measurable acceptance gates are named.
- The artefact contains no unsupported coverage, pricing, reserve, payment or treaty conclusion.

## Artefact-specific acceptance criteria

- Every incident scenario names the specific manual_mode value from data/continuity_requirements.csv it falls back to, never a generic "manual process" (CTRL-IR-01).
- The confirmed ransomware containment (INJ-080) scenario reflects its actual current parameters (PolicyCore-IN read-only, ClaimsImages offline, degraded payment mode) exactly as recorded, not a hypothetical variant (CTRL-IR-02).
- Every scenario's evidence-preservation step specifies what must never be altered during containment, consistent with knowledge/AI_INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md's "preserve evidence" requirement (CTRL-IR-03).
- No recovery-validation step permits resuming full AI-assisted operation without first re-verifying current authorization and model-artefact integrity, reusing (not bypassing) CTRL-DGL-01/CTRL-DGL-03 (CTRL-IR-04).

| A-04 | Evidence preservation during containment means the raw evidence a workflow was processing at the moment of containment (claim documents, evidence-reconciliation state, audit trail) must not be altered, deleted, or reprocessed until the incident investigation confirms it is safe to do so; this is stricter than simply "keep backups." | knowledge/AI_INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md (EVID-112, "preserve evidence" requirement) | Altering or reprocessing evidence mid-incident could destroy the forensic trail needed to determine what the ransomware/leakage/denial-of-wallet event actually affected. | CISO, Group Chief Claims Officer | During any active incident |
