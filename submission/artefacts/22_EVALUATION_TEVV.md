# 22 Evaluation Tevv

## Purpose

Golden, edge, adversarial, fairness, reliability and cost tests with release gates for the three workflows, built on the 18 public fixtures, 96 inject test obligations and 12 required test families already supplied in evaluation/, directly closing non-compensable gate 8's zero-sub-claim gap identified in 21_ASSURANCE_CASE.md.

## Acceptance criteria

- The artefact is evidence-led and references supplied paths, record locators and inject IDs.
- Facts, assumptions, inference, conflict, decision and residual risk are distinguishable.
- Temporal, jurisdictional, authorization and authority boundaries are explicit where relevant.
- Owners, approvals, review triggers and measurable acceptance gates are named.
- The artefact contains no unsupported coverage, pricing, reserve, payment or treaty conclusion.

## Artefact-specific acceptance criteria

- Every one of the 18 public fixtures (EV-01 through EV-18) is traced to a contract, an evidence bundle and a release-gate decision, directly closing gate 8's zero-sub-claim status from 21_ASSURANCE_CASE.md (CTRL-TEVV-01).
- Every one of the 12 required test families in evaluation/EVALUATION_PLAN.md has at least one concrete test row, not only a category name (CTRL-TEVV-02).
- Every `fail-closed` release-gate inject (per evaluation/INJECT_TEST_OBLIGATIONS.csv) has an explicit "prohibited action blocked" assertion in its test row; `review-required` gates route to a named human role instead (CTRL-TEVV-03).
- No release gate reports only an aggregate pass rate; every subgroup-relevant test (fairness, multilingual) reports per-subgroup results, consistent with knowledge/FAIRNESS_AND_ADVERSE_ACTION.md's subgroup-evaluation requirement (CTRL-TEVV-04).

| Report only an aggregate pass/fail rate across all 18 fixtures, without per-fixture or per-subgroup detail | Simplest summary metric | Directly hides the confirmed fairness/multilingual gaps (A-04); a single aggregate number cannot distinguish a fail-closed violation from a review-required routing miss | Lowest reporting cost, highest risk of concealing exactly the failures this package requires disclosed | Reversible but wastes the diagnostic value of the fixture set | Rejected. | Claim all 18 fixtures "pass" without actually executing them against implemented code, since no submission/src exists yet | Appears to close gate 8 immediately | Would be a fabricated result — no implementation exists to execute against; directly contradicts the evidence-led discipline established since Step 1 and would misrepresent the submission's actual state at the final defence | Lowest effort, highest credibility risk if challenged (a panel could ask to see the passing run) | N/A — a fabricated pass cannot be honestly reversed | Rejected outright, consistent with 21_ASSURANCE_CASE.md's A-02 honest-claim-strength discipline. |
