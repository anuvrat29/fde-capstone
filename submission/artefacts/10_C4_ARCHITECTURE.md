# 10 C4 Architecture

## Purpose

Context, container, component and critical-flow architecture for Workflow A (coverage/claim/fraud evidence reconciliation), Workflow B (underwriting/pricing decision support) and Workflow C (catastrophe/reinsurance planning support), grounded in the twelve source systems in case/SOURCE_SYSTEM_FACT_PACK.md and the bounded fraud-ring graph component qualified in template 08.

## Acceptance criteria

- The artefact is evidence-led and references supplied paths, record locators and inject IDs.
- Facts, assumptions, inference, conflict, decision and residual risk are distinguishable.
- Temporal, jurisdictional, authorization and authority boundaries are explicit where relevant.
- Owners, approvals, review triggers and measurable acceptance gates are named.
- The artefact contains no unsupported coverage, pricing, reserve, payment or treaty conclusion.

## Artefact-specific acceptance criteria

- Every container that writes to a system of record (PayFlow, ClaimSphere, PolicyCore-IN, ReSure) has an explicit trust-boundary note stating "no AI write permission" or the equivalent human-approval gate, consistent with case/SOURCE_SYSTEM_FACT_PACK.md's PayFlow caveat (CTRL-C4-01).
- The fraud-ring graph component qualified in 08_KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH_DECISION.md appears as exactly one bounded component with a stated failure mode of "abstain and route to human investigator", never "auto-decline" (CTRL-C4-02).
- Every one of the 9 currently uncontrolled non-functional requirements from 09_REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.md (REQ-NF-01, 08, 10–18) is assigned to at least one architectural element as a named responsibility, even where the element is not yet built (CTRL-C4-03).
- No container or component diagram implies a single global source-of-truth; each data-handling row states which fact types the system is authoritative for and which it is not (CTRL-C4-04).

## Alternatives and trade-offs

| Option | Benefits | Risks | Cost/complexity | Reversibility | Decision and rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single monolithic service handling all three workflows plus the fraud-ring graph as shared internal modules | Simpler deployment topology; one codebase to secure and version | Blurs workflow-specific prohibited-action boundaries (contradicts the three-service decision already made in 04_PRODUCT_SERVICE_BLUEPRINT.md); would expose the fraud-ring graph to Workflow B without a demonstrated query need, violating A-04 | Lower initial build cost, high governance/audit cost (cannot cleanly disable one workflow without affecting others) | Costly to reverse once workflows are entangled in one deployable | Rejected. Directly contradicts the per-workflow AI-disabled continuity requirement (REQ-NF-16) and the bounded-graph-scope decision in template 08. |
| Three independent workflow services with a shared but access-scoped fraud-ring graph component, current-authorization gate and model-verification gate as reusable internal components | Preserves per-workflow independent kill-switch capability; reuses the two verification gates without duplicating logic; graph access is scoped per A-04 | Requires disciplined internal access control so Workflow B cannot silently gain graph access as the system evolves (same scope-creep risk flagged in 08's Open issues) | Medium — three deployables plus three shared internal components | Reversible; any one workflow or shared component can be disabled independently | Selected. Matches the three-workflow mandate (case/INTEGRATED_CASE.md Section 4), the bounded-graph decision (08), and the AI-disabled continuity requirement without duplicating the verification-gate logic three times. |
| Direct AI-to-system-of-record write access for PayFlow/ClaimSphere/PolicyCore-IN to reduce human-approval latency | Fastest possible cycle time, directly addresses the board's cycle-time target (INJ-001) | Violates non-compensable gate 1 outright (no autonomous binding/pricing/reserve/payment); PayFlow's "No AI write permission is allowed" caveat is explicit in case/SOURCE_SYSTEM_FACT_PACK.md | N/A — not a viable option | N/A | Rejected outright. Not evaluated further; recorded only to demonstrate the option was considered and excluded on non-compensable-gate grounds, consistent with 01_BUSINESS_CASE.md's treatment of full automation. |
