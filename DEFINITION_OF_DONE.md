# Definition of Done

The capstone is complete only when a qualified independent reviewer can reproduce, inspect and challenge the submission without oral knowledge from its builders.

## Product and business

- A quantified baseline, no-AI alternative, benefits model and stop criteria exist.
- Intended purpose, users, affected persons, prohibited actions and decision rights are explicit.
- All three mandatory workflows are demonstrable through happy, conflict, abstention and recovery paths.

## Domain and evidence

- Policy, party, role, coverage, claim, loss event, peril, location, model, treaty and evidence concepts are separated.
- Temporal, jurisdictional and source-authority rules are explicit and testable.
- Every material output cites evidence, provenance, version, timestamp and authority status.
- All 96 injects are mapped to requirements, controls, tests and residual-risk ownership.

## Engineering and architecture

- C4 views, integration contracts and at least 12 meaningful ADRs are complete.
- One-command setup, run, test, evaluation and reset paths are documented.
- Structured outputs reject prohibited fields and unauthorized side effects.
- The final package runs offline with no secrets, cloud keys or undeclared dependencies.

## Safety, governance and assurance

- Current authorization, purpose limitation, tenant isolation and human approval are enforced.
- Prompt injection, tool poisoning, privilege escalation, replay, data leakage and denial-of-wallet tests pass.
- Fairness, multilingual fidelity, accessibility, contestability and adverse-action controls are evidenced.
- Regulatory applicability distinguishes binding requirement, supervisory expectation, internal policy and design choice.
- Manual fallback, model substitution, incident response, vendor exit and retirement are executable.

## Evaluation and economics

- All 18 public fixtures execute against versioned contracts.
- Accuracy, evidence fidelity, citation quality, abstention, security, fairness, reliability, latency and cost gates are reported.
- Token and unit economics are measured per successful reviewed outcome, not per raw model call.

## Defence

- The team can trace any panel-selected inject to evidence, requirement, design decision, test result and residual risk.
- The team states clearly what the system is not authorized to decide or execute.
